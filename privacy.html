1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to promedhospital.co.in (the “Website”), operated by ProMED Hospital, 3781, 13th Cross, Banashankari 2nd Stage, Bangalore, Karnataka (“Promed”, “we”, “us”). It describes how we handle personal data shared with us through the Website, by telephone or over WhatsApp in connection with an enquiry or appointment request.

We process personal data in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under it, and other applicable Indian law. Clinical records created during your treatment at the hospital are governed by applicable medical record-keeping regulations and by the hospital’s patient policies. This policy does not cover them.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Appointment requests: your name, phone number, preferred department, preferred date and any message you choose to include.
  • Calls and messages: details you share when you phone the hospital or message us on WhatsApp.
  • Health information: only what you choose to share when describing a concern. Please share no more than you need to for us to arrange your visit.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider records standard technical data when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited and the date and time of access. We use this only to keep the Website secure and working properly. We do not use advertising or behavioural-tracking cookies.

3. How we use your information

We use personal data only for the following purposes:

  • to respond to your enquiry and to schedule, confirm or reschedule appointments;
  • to contact you about your visit, including reminders and follow-up care;
  • to help with insurance or cashless-treatment formalities, when you ask us to;
  • to maintain, secure and improve the Website;
  • to meet our legal, regulatory and medico-legal obligations.

We process your data on the basis of your consent, which you give when you submit a request or contact us, or where the law otherwise permits. We will never sell your personal data or use it for third-party marketing.

4. Sharing & disclosure

Your information is shared within Promed only with the people who need it to coordinate your care. We disclose it outside Promed only in these cases:

  • Service providers who host or support our Website and communication systems, under an obligation to keep it confidential;
  • Insurers and third-party administrators, when you ask us to process a cashless or reimbursement claim;
  • Referring or consulting clinicians, where needed for your treatment and with your consent;
  • Authorities, when disclosure is required by law, court order or a public-health obligation.

5. Cookies & third-party services

The Website itself does not set tracking cookies. Some pages include content from third parties that may collect data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Maps, used to show our location;
  • Google Fonts, used to display the Website’s typefaces;
  • WhatsApp, if you choose to message us through it.

We encourage you to read the privacy policies of these providers. Links to external websites are provided for convenience, and we are not responsible for their privacy practices.

6. Data retention

We keep enquiry and appointment data only for as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected for, or as long as the law requires. After that, we delete it securely or anonymise it. Medical records are retained for the periods required by applicable regulations.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. These include encrypted (HTTPS) connections, restricted staff access and confidentiality obligations. No online transmission is ever completely secure, but we work hard to protect your information and will act promptly if a breach occurs, including notifying you and the authorities where required.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • request a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is used;
  • ask us to correct, complete or update inaccurate information;
  • ask us to erase your data when it is no longer needed, unless we must keep it by law;
  • withdraw your consent at any time (this does not affect processing already carried out);
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity;
  • raise a grievance with us and, if unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Grievance Officer using the details below.

9. Children

Appointments for patients under 18 should be requested by a parent or lawful guardian. We process a child’s data only with verifiable consent from a parent or guardian, and only for the child’s care.

10. Grievance officer

If you have questions, concerns or complaints about how your personal data is handled, please contact:

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or in the law. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Please review it periodically.
